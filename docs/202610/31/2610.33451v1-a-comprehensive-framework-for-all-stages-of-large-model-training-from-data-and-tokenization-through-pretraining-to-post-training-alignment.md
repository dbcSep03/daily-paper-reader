## 摘要
大语言模型（LLMs）与多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）的能力源于完整训练流程的协同作用，包括数据与分词、预训练以及后训练（监督微调、偏好对齐、强化学习和持续优化），而非任何单一阶段。现有综述大多采用单阶段或单技术视角，将相关研究视为孤立系统，缺乏统一的阶段划分和横向可比的基础。本文提出统一的阶段划分和六维分析框架，涵盖数据、目标函数、优化策略、系统约束、评估以及风险与治理。我们系统梳理了2017至2026年的395篇核心参考文献，并给出跨阶段效应的概念图：预训练对后训练的前向约束，以及后训练对数据和预训练的反向赋能。我们得出三项核心发现。第一，缩放定律的可比性取决于数据质量与混合，而这是一个长期被忽视的变量。第二，偏好对齐方法（RLHF、DPO）与带可验证奖励的推理强化学习重新界定了后训练边界，而Pass@1与Pass@K之争仍未解决。第三，多模态训练在分词器设计、训练目标和评估方面对纯文本范式构成结构性挑战。本文提供跨阶段效应图、方法族图谱、实践决策树和至少10个可验证的开放问题，并为各阶段的专题子综述提供可扩展骨架。截至检索日期，同行评审证据（A+B）约占395篇参考文献的79%。

## Abstract
The capabilities of large language models (LLMs) and multimodal LLMs (MLLMs) arise from the synergy of the full training pipeline, including data and tokenization, pretraining, and post-training (supervised fine-tuning, preference alignment, reinforcement learning, and continual optimization), rather than any individual stage. Existing surveys mostly adopt a single-stage or single-technique perspective, treating related research as isolated systems without unified stage division or a horizontally comparable basis.   This paper proposes a unified stage division and a six-dimensional analytical framework covering data, objective function, optimization strategy, system constraints, evaluation, and risk and governance. We systematically review 395 core references from 2017 to 2026, and present a conceptual map of cross-stage effects: forward constraints of pretraining on post-training, and reverse empowerment of post-training on data and pretraining.   We derive three core findings. First, the comparability of scaling laws depends on data quality and mixture, a long-neglected variable. Second, preference alignment methods (RLHF, DPO) and reasoning reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards redefine post-training boundaries, while the Pass@1 versus Pass@K debate remains unresolved. Third, multimodal training poses structural challenges to the pure-text paradigm in tokenizer design, training objectives, and evaluation.   The paper provides a cross-stage effects diagram, a method-family map, a practical decision tree, and at least 10 verifiable open problems, with an extensible skeleton for focused sub-surveys per stage. As of the retrieval date, peer-reviewed evidence (A+B) accounts for approximately 79% of the 395 references.
